# F3 — business onboarding, settings and scoped team access

Version 0.3.0 extends F1/F2; no dependency/framework replacements. All new workflows are mock-complete against proposed contracts; none is API-integrated or staging-verified.

- Six-step save/resume application with section/final validation, metadata-only test document upload/replace/remove, review lock, changes/resubmission and status timeline.
- Eligibility approval separated from account request and simulated zero-balance provisioning. Existing banking fixtures remain clearly independent.
- Versioned operating profile edits; legal identity read-only. Mock sign-in policy and scoped own-session revocation with enforcement limitations visible.
- Stateful invitations (create/resend/revoke/expire/accept simulation), member roles/status, readable scope matrix, self/last-admin protection and explicit developer entitlement intersection.
- Permission-aware shared shell and guarded banking routes; lazy F3 routes, reusable forms/inline feedback/version notices/audit components and mobile layouts.
- Same typed JSON/multipart HTTP boundary and MSW; no page-local fixtures or successful-toast-only actions. Development reset clears related business records.

Kenya-only fields and missing team/settings screens are documented assumptions. Review, KYB, mail, authentication enforcement and account opening are simulations, not integrations. Payment approvals/execution remain F6, production APIs/credentials F7, contacts and billing F4 next. Verification results and exact Windows commands are in VERIFICATION.md and README.md.
